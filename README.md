# IAU OAD – Science Forum 2026 kiosk

A touch-screen kiosk that replaces printed flyers for the IAU Office of Astronomy for Development (OAD). Visitors spin an orbit of OAD activities and open short flyers. They can then email their chosen flyers to themselves, or scan a QR code to join the mailing list or Discord.

**Live page:** https://acidian.github.io/IAU_OAD_ScienceForum2026/

Status: MVP v0.1. Email runs in demo mode (a preview, nothing is sent). All activity text is placeholder until approved.

Setup, editing and email details: [README_v0.1_2026-11-25.md](README_v0.1_2026-11-25.md).
