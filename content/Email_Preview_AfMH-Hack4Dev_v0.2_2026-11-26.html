Flagship 2 – Astronomy for Mental Health

Looking up together: the night sky for calm, perspective and connection.

Since 2020, the OAD’s Astronomy for Mental Health flagship has used simple, low-cost stargazing activities to support wellbeing in communities. Guided experiences under the night sky help people find calm, a new sense of perspective and connection with others. It supports wellbeing and is not therapy.

  • Open guidelines, free for anyone to use (v1 2023, v2 2025)
  • Facilitator training, including a free online course, and a global community of practice
  • Peer-reviewed findings with Stellenbosch University (IJERPH, 2026), among 9 publications so far
  • Dark-sky retreats, including in Sutherland
  • Wellbeing, not therapy: this is not counselling, assessment, diagnosis, treatment or crisis support, and it does not replace them.

Flagship 3 – Astro 4 Skills: Hack4Dev

Turning young talent into data-science opportunity.

Hack4Dev runs hands-on data science hackathons. Teams of students and young professionals use astronomy-grade data skills to tackle real development challenges. Trained local organisers run 4-day hackathons at their own institutions, so each cycle trains the people who run the next.

  • Launched in 2022 by the OAD with IDIA, DARA, AfAS and BRICS partners
  • 350+ participants selected and trained from about 2,000 applications
  • Women’s participation up from under 20% to 41%
  • 2026: 22 hackathons in 13 countries, with open challenges on GitHub

“Astronomy has always been a data science.”
